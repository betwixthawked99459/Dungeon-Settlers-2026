## 🕹️ About

Dungeon Settlers is a dark-fantasy strategy game combining colony simulation with dungeon crawling. Lead a Guild expedition, build a settlement in barren land, provide food and shelter, and prepare settlers for dangerous dungeon expeditions. Gather resources, develop your characters, and overcome challenging tactical combat as you work to destroy the source of the calamities. The game is planned for Windows Early Access on September 4, 2026.

Dungeon Settlers is for players who enjoy hardcore colony management, resource planning, character development, and strategically demanding dungeon crawlers.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏕️ **Build a Settlement** — Establish and expand a settlement in barren land.
- 🍞 **Manage Resources** — Provide food, shelter, equipment, and supplies for your people.
- 🔬 **Research and Produce** — Unlock new buildings, equipment, and production options.
- ⚔️ **Lead Expeditions** — Prepare a party of settlers and guide them into the dungeon.
- 🗡️ **Explore Dungeons** — Descend through dangerous dungeon regions to gather vital resources.
- 🎯 **Use Tactical Combat** — Overcome challenging encounters through careful strategy.
- 📈 **Develop Characters** — Train settlers, improve their abilities, and build specialized parties.
- 💀 **Face Permanent Consequences** — Make expedition decisions where failure can permanently cost valuable settlers.
